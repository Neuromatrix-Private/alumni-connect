# SIES Alumni Connect: invitation direction

Design proposal, 4 November 2026. The SRS remains the authority. This is a canvas design, not an implemented website or approved institutional identity.

## Decision

The first forest/editorial concept is retained in `design.pen`. The revised concept uses **The page is the object**, dealt by the installed Taste Skill with seed **1716343116**. Barlow Condensed and Barlow, frost/slate surfaces, and one teal action colour replace the first direction.

On this page, **the SIES Memory Chest and the invitation to return to an SIES institution become a digital alumni invitation, with photographs and a reply section**.

The specific content inventory is: SIES; South Indian Education Society; one network across institutions; institution/programme/batch affiliations; the Memory Chest; Unforgettable Mentors; Familiar Faces; alumni-to-stewardship; Alumnite as a managed event; the Alumni Secretariat; campus visit requests; verification separate from directory consent. These come from the supplied SRS, not invented proof.

The idea is more specific than a generic library photograph, although the invitation format could also serve another alumni network. Approved SIES archives and voices will be needed to make the final site unmistakably its own.

The proposed live interaction is an inquiry-topic selector with a changing explanatory panel. Topics use SRS inquiry categories: verification, reunions, campus visits and contributions. The canvas shows states; no submission or persistence is simulated.

## Dealt-section changes

- Showcase: replaced output slips with Square plate, spare text. There are no approved alumni letters or memory submissions to reproduce.
- About: replaced Founder's letter with Side facts, wide paragraph. No approved President's letter, portrait or signature was supplied.
- List: replaced Printed menu sheet with One item per poster line. There are four participation routes, not 12–24 priced services.
- The card has straight edges; decorative scallops, diamonds and simulated paper wear would add no SIES meaning.
- The full invitation is visible on the canvas. Browser-fold cropping and the proposed footer reveal belong to implementation, not clipped design content.

## Scope and verification

Deliverables are editable Pen.dev desktop, tablet and mobile frames, token variables, reusable component examples and this theme reference. No framework, backend, authentication, payments or publication is part of this task. Browser inspector and motion checks do not run against a `.pen` canvas; use Pen.dev layout diagnostics and rendered screenshots, and carry browser checks forward to implementation.

Institutional records, President content, events, statistics, contact details and historical assertions require SIES approval. Payment actions remain inquiry-only until the SRS gates are satisfied. Public directory previews must not expose unconsented people or contacts.
