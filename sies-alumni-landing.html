Welcome Home to Your Alma Mater!

How wonderful to have you back!

Years may have passed. Life may have taken you to distant cities, different countries and along paths you never imagined. But somewhere in your heart, SIES has always had a special place. And here, at SIES, you have never been forgotten.

You may have left SIES years ago. SIES has never left you.

Welcome back. We are delighted to have you home!

With love and affection,

Your SIES Family

From the President's Desk

A Word from the President

It gives me great pleasure to extend a warm and heartfelt greeting to each of you from SIES and welcome you to the SIES Alumni portal – your digital home that celebrates our shared journey of learning, discovery and transformation.

As past students of the various SIES institutions, we are the torchbearers of the legacy that now spans far and wide. Our collective footsteps have laid the foundation on which SIES has grown leaps and bounds over the years and flourished. Each one of us has carried a part of SIES legacies into the world, our careers, our communities and families.

The reunions are more than a chance to catch up. They are reminders that no matter how far we have travelled or how much our lifestyles have changed, we still share something that cannot erase, which is the bond of growing together. It’s a legacy of determination, innovation and unwavering spirit that we must carry forward for generations to come.

The Alumni network is a testament to the bonds we forged in classrooms, play areas, libraries and corridors of our institutions. It’s reflection of the dreams we nurtured, the challenges we overcame and the victories we celebrated. Each of us has a unique story to tell and together, our stories paint a vivid canvas of the shared SIES experience.

Till the end of the 20th century, we were just about four institutions in the SIES family. Today, we have expanded to eight campuses across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai housing 20 institutions. All the institutions of higher learning except the School of Pharmaceutical Sciences have been conferred ‘Autonomous Status’ by the Government of India. The School of Pharmaceutical Sciences is in its infancy and is just two years old.

The SIES College of Arts, Science and Commerce (Empowered Autonomous) would soon become an University approved by the Government of India. Several new institutions of higher learning particularly in Health Sciences and AI are on the anvil.

In about five years’ time, the Society would celebrate the centenary of its institutional journey. Now is the time to remain actively engaged with the institutions and support the Mission of SIES to be One of the most Admired Academic institutions in the country and beyond.

With warm regards,
Dr. V. Shankar
President, South Indian Education Society

SIES INSTITUTIONS

Our Campuses & Institutions

A legacy of learning, growth and lifelong connections.

SIES High School Matunga

SIES High School, Matunga

“Where Learning Shapes Character.”

Founded on 6 June 1932 with just six students in a modest garage, SIES High School is the proud birthplace of the SIES educational family. A reputed English-medium, co-educational institution affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board (SSC), it is known for academic excellence, discipline and strong cultural values. For generations of students, its classrooms have nurtured knowledge, character, friendships and memories that endure a lifetime.

SIES ASC Sion

SIES College of Arts, Science & Commerce, Sion (West)

“Discover Potential. Shape Possibilities.”

Established in 1960, this flagship SIES institution has earned an enviable reputation for academic excellence, research and holistic education. Awarded Empowered Autonomous status and accredited 'A' Grade by NAAC (CGPA 3.02), it was also honoured with the University of Mumbai's Best College Award in 2010. Generations of students cherish its rich traditions, inspiring teachers and lifelong friendships.

Plan a Visit ›
SIES APJ Memorial High School

SIES (Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam Memorial) High School, Ghatkopar

“Dream. Discover. Achieve.”

Established in 1962 as North Mumbai Welfare Society's High School, this reputed institution became part of SIES in 2017 and was renamed in honour of Bharat Ratna Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, former Principal Patron of SIES. Known for its academic excellence, scientific curiosity and holistic education, the school recorded an impressive 98.84% SSC pass rate in 2024–25. With a proud legacy of nurturing young minds, it continues to inspire generations to dream, discover and excel.

SIES ICE

SIES Institute of Comprehensive Education

“Nurturing Teachers. Shaping Generations.”

Established in 1980, SIES Institute of Comprehensive Education has evolved into a respected centre for professional training in Early Childhood Education, Special Education and Counselling. Offering specialised programmes, including University of Mumbai-affiliated courses, the institute combines academic excellence with practical training, innovation and compassionate service. Through its commitment to nurturing educators, counsellors and professionals, SIES ICE has touched countless lives, inspiring generations of alumni who proudly carry forward its legacy of knowledge, care and social responsibility.

SIES CE Sion

SIES College of Commerce & Economics, Sion (East)

“Knowledge for Enterprise. Values for Life.”

Established in 1989 and inaugurated by the then Vice-President of India, Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma, the college has grown into a respected centre for commerce and professional education in Mumbai. An autonomous, NAAC-accredited institution, it offers diverse programmes in Commerce, Management, Finance, Information Technology and emerging disciplines such as Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. With its emphasis on academic excellence, innovation and career development, the college has nurtured generations of professionals, entrepreneurs and achievers who proudly carry their SIES legacy into the world.

SIES COMS

SIES College of Management Studies (SIESCOMS)

“Inspiring Minds. Shaping Leaders.”

Established in 1995, SIES College of Management Studies has earned a distinguished reputation for quality management and technology education. An autonomous institution offering University of Mumbai-affiliated MMS, MCA and doctoral programmes, it combines academic excellence, research, industry engagement and ethical values. With NBA-accredited programmes and a strong record of corporate placements, SIESCOMS has nurtured generations of accomplished managers, technology professionals and entrepreneurs who proudly carry forward its legacy of leadership, innovation and lifelong friendships.

Plan a Visit ›
SIES IIEM

SIES Indian Institute of Environment Management (SIES IIEM), Navi Mumbai

“Rooted in Knowledge. Connected by Purpose. Advancing Sustainability.”

Established in 1999, SIES Indian Institute of Environment Management has emerged as a reputed centre for environmental education, research, consultancy and sustainable development. Recognised by the Government of India's Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR) and certified to ISO 9001:2015 standards, the institute undertakes significant research in pollution control, water management, biodiversity and climate change. Through collaborations with government agencies, industry and research organisations, it nurtures environmental professionals and researchers committed to building a cleaner, greener and more sustainable future.

Plan a Visit ›
SIES School of Packaging

SIES School of Packaging (Packaging Technology Centre), Navi Mumbai

“Innovating Packaging. Sustaining Progress.”

Established in 2001, SIES School of Packaging has emerged as one of India's reputed centres of excellence in packaging education, research, testing and consultancy. Recognised as a Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation by the Government of India's DSIR, it is also ISO 9001:2015 certified for packaging testing and evaluation services. Recognised as a Gold Standard Institution for 2025–26 by Outlook-ICARE, the institute enjoys international professional affiliations and strong industry partnerships. Its alumni contribute to packaging innovation, sustainability and technological advancement across India and beyond.

SIES GST Nerul

SIES Graduate School of Technology, Nerul

“Engineering Progress. Enriching Lives.”

Established in 2002, SIES Graduate School of Technology has emerged as a leading engineering institution in Navi Mumbai, recognised for academic excellence, innovation and industry-oriented education. Granted autonomous status in 2024 and accredited A+ by NAAC (CGPA 3.28), it has earned NBA accreditation for key engineering programmes and prestigious quality awards. Ranked 48th among engineering colleges in India by the Times Engineering Survey 2024, the institution continues to nurture accomplished engineers, innovators and entrepreneurs, fostering lifelong friendships and pride in their alma mater.

SIES SBS

SIES School of Business Studies (SIES SBS), Nerul

“Leadership with Values. Enterprise with Purpose.”

Carrying forward a distinguished management education legacy dating to 1996, SIES School of Business Studies became an independent institution in 2020. Offering AICTE-approved postgraduate management programmes, including specialised studies in Pharmaceutical Management and Biotechnology, it has earned recognition for industry-oriented education, innovation and international academic accreditation. Its pioneering contributions to sector-specific management education, strong corporate engagement and emphasis on ethical leadership have nurtured generations of business professionals and entrepreneurs who cherish their enduring association with the SIES.

Plan a Visit ›
SIES SPS Nerul

SIES School of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Nerul

“Advancing Science. Improving Lives.”

Established in 2024, SIES School of Pharmaceutical Sciences is a promising new addition to the SIES family, dedicated to excellence in pharmaceutical education, research and innovation. Offering D.Pharm and B.Pharm programmes in a modern, purpose-built campus, the institution combines academic rigour with advanced laboratories, industry engagement and practical training. Its Pharmaceutical Formulation Innovation, Technology & Engineering Centre, inaugurated in 2025, further strengthens its research and industry interface. Rooted in the SIES tradition of service, the school aspires to nurture future pharmacists, researchers and healthcare innovators committed to improving lives.
